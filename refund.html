14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of buying Pro, for any reason.

How to ask for a refund

Email support@kelvora.app with the subject "Refund request", your order ID and the email address you used to buy. You can also use the contact form and choose "Refund request".

When you'll get your money

We confirm refunds within 2 working days. The money goes back to your original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days depending on your bank.

After a refund

Your Pro licence is deactivated and the app returns to the free plan. Your chats and keys stay on your computer.

Exceptions

Refunds after 14 days are at our discretion. We may refuse refunds where a licence has clearly been shared or misused.

Charges from AI providers

We can only refund what you paid Kelvora. Charges from AI providers for paid API keys are handled by those providers.
