Features

Built for people who use AI every day

Kelvora handles the parts of working with many AI providers that waste your time: limits, switching, keys and scattered chat history.

Automatic fallback

When a provider errors, times out or hits a limit, Kelvora retries the same message on the next model in your list.

Fast model picking

Kelvora measures response times on your connection and prefers the providers that answer quickest for you.

Side-by-side compare

Send a prompt to up to three models at once and keep the reply you like best.

Local key vault

Keys are encrypted with your device's password store and never leave your computer.

Projects and history

Group chats into projects, search past conversations and export them as Markdown or JSON.

Usage meter

See how much of each provider's free quota you've used today and when it resets.

Bring any key

Free tiers, trial credits or paid keys all work together in one routing list.

Files and images

Attach PDFs, text files and images. Kelvora sends them only to models that support them.

Keyboard first

Open a new chat, switch models and search history without touching the mouse.

How routing works

One message, the best available model

Every time you send a message, Kelvora checks your routing list from top to bottom. It skips any provider whose daily limit is used up, then sends the message to the first one that's ready. If that provider fails, the message moves to the next one automatically.

  • You set the order, or let Kelvora sort by speed
  • The reply shows which provider answered
  • Failed attempts never use up your other quotas

Privacy by design

Nothing passes through us

Kelvora is a desktop app, not a web service. Your messages travel from your computer straight to the AI provider you're routed to. We don't run a server that could read, store or sell them.
