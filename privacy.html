Privacy policy

Kelvora is built so that we collect as little about you as possible. This policy explains what we do and don't collect.

Last updated: 25 October 2026

SummaryWhat we collectAI providersPaymentsYour rightsHow long we keep dataChildrenChanges to this policyContact

Summary

  • Your API keys and chat history are stored only on your computer.
  • Your messages go directly to the AI provider you are routed to, not through our servers.
  • We collect purchase details only if you buy Pro, and only what is needed to issue and verify your licence.
  • We do not sell personal data.

What we collect

Data that never reaches us

API keys, chat messages, files you attach and your conversation history stay on your device. Keys are encrypted using your operating system's secure storage.

Data we do collect

  • Licence details (Pro only): your name, email address, order ID and, if you provide it, your GSTIN, to issue invoices and activate licences.
  • Support messages: what you send us when you contact support.
  • Update checks: when Kelvora checks for updates, it sends the app version and operating system type. No personal identifiers are included.
  • Crash reports: off by default. If you turn them on, they include technical details of the error but no chat content or keys.

AI providers

When you send a message, it goes from your computer to the AI provider that Kelvora routes it to. That provider processes your message under its own terms and privacy policy. Please review the policies of the providers whose keys you add.

Payments

Payments for Pro are handled by our payment processor. We do not see or store your full card or bank details.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable laws, you can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal data we hold about you. Email our support team and we will respond within 30 days.

How long we keep data

We keep licence and invoice records for as long as required by tax law (currently up to 8 years in India). Support messages are deleted 24 months after the conversation ends.

Children

Kelvora is not intended for children under 18 without the consent of a parent or guardian.

Changes to this policy

If we make significant changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and mention it in the app's release notes.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@kelvora.app.
