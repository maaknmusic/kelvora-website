General

What is Kelvora?

A desktop app that lets you chat with AI models from many providers in one place. It uses your own API keys and switches to another provider automatically when one hits its limit.

Is Kelvora really free?

Yes. The free plan costs nothing and has no time limit. It works with the free tiers that many AI providers offer. Pro is an optional one-time upgrade.

Which providers does it support?

More than 40, including most major AI model providers and many OpenAI-compatible services. You can also add a custom endpoint if your provider isn't listed.

Keys and privacy

Where do I get API keys?

Most AI providers give you a key after you sign up on their developer website. Kelvora's setup screen links to each one and shows where to paste the key.

Does Kelvora see my conversations?

No. Messages go from your computer straight to the provider that answers. Chats and keys are stored only on your device.

What happens to my data at the AI provider?

Each provider handles your messages under its own privacy policy. Kelvora shows which provider answered each message so you always know where it went.

Using the app

What happens when every free limit is used up?

Kelvora tells you which limit resets next and when, so you can wait or add another key.

Can I use it offline?

You can read past chats offline. Sending new messages needs an internet connection, since the models run on the providers' servers.

How do I move to a new computer?

Export your chats from Settings, install Kelvora on the new computer and import them. Re-enter your keys there; they are never exported, for safety.
